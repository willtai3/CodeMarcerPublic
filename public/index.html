You vibecoded the app. Hire the engineer who ships it.

Engineers list their hourly rate and fixed-price packages. Pick one, send a request, and pay the engineer directly with whatever they accept.

  1. Find an engineer whose skills fit your app.
  2. Choose a package or hourly work and send your request.
  3. They contact you by email or phone, you pay them directly, and they mark the job complete.
